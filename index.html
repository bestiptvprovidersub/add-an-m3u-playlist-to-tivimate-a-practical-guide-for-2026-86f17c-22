Step-by-step: Add an M3U playlist to TiviMate

  1. Open TiviMate on your device. The main screen appears.
  2. Go to Settings. Open the menu by pressing the remote’s menu button (three horizontal lines or gear icon). Select “Settings” from the list.
  3. Enter the Playlists section. Inside Settings, choose “Playlists”. This is where all your IPTV sources are managed.
  4. Tap “Add playlist”. A new screen gives you two input methods: “M3U URL” and “Xstream”. Select “M3U URL”.
  5. Paste your M3U link. Use the on-screen keyboard to enter or paste the full M3U URL you received from your provider. Double-check that there are no extra spaces.
  6. Give the playlist a name. Below the URL field, type a name you will easily recognize (for example “My SubTV Playlist”). This name appears on the left menu.
  7. Tap “Next” and then “Save”. TiviMate will download the playlist. Depending on the number of channels, this takes a few seconds.
  8. Select the playlist to start watching. Go back to the main screen. Open the left menu (press left on your remote). Your new playlist is listed there. Click on it to browse channels.

If a step fails – common fixes

Playlist does not load or shows “Invalid URL”.
Check that you copied the full URL – it usually starts with http:// or https://. If your provider requires a user-agent or extra headers, contact their WhatsApp support for the correct format.

Channels are buffering.
Go to “Settings > Playlists > your playlist > User-Agent” and leave it blank unless told otherwise. Then open “Settings > Network” and set DNS to Cloudflare (1.1.1.1). Under “Settings > Playback > Buffer size”, choose 5,000 ms. This matches SubTV IP’s recommended anti-throttling setup.

App crashes or freezes.
Make sure you are running TiviMate 5.x and your device has the latest firmware. If using a Fire TV Stick, clear the cache: “Settings > Applications > Manage installed applications > TiviMate > Clear cache”.

For persistent issues, many users find helpful Q&A about IPTV setup on Quora Streaming. Technical deep-dives on streaming protocols are available from MIT Technology Review.

FAQ: Adding M3U playlists to TiviMate

What is an M3U playlist?

An M3U playlist is a text file or URL that lists channel names and their streaming links. TiviMate reads this list and shows the channels in a convenient grid.

Can I add more than one M3U playlist?

Yes, TiviMate supports multiple playlists. Repeat the steps above for each new M3U URL. You can switch between them from the left menu.

Why does my M3U playlist not appear after adding it?

The most common cause is a slow internet connection or incorrect URL. Check that the URL starts with http:// and contains no extra characters. If it still fails, restart TiviMate and try again.

How do I update an M3U playlist without re-adding it?

Open “Settings > Playlists”, select your playlist, then tap “Update”. TiviMate fetches the latest channel list from the same URL.

Is an M3U URL the same as an Xtream code?

No. M3U is a direct link to a playlist file. Xtream requires a separate username, password, and server URL. If your provider gave you Xtream codes, choose “Xstream” in step 4 instead of “M3U URL”.

Can I use a free M3U playlist from the internet?

Free public M3U playlists are often unreliable, contain outdated links, and may violate copyright rules. A paid subscription like SubTV IP ensures stable 4K streams, instant activation, and WhatsApp support.

What should I do if my playlist works but channels are missing?

Some playlists group content by category. Open the left menu and scroll through all groups (Movies, Sports, News, etc.). If channels are still missing, contact your provider’s WhatsApp support for a refreshed URL.
